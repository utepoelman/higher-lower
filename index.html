📖 Game Rules & Probability Strategy

🃏 Core Rules & Points Ladder

  • Cards are drawn without replacement from a deck numbered 1 to 100 (no ties are possible).
  • Start with 0 points. Predict whether the next card will be HIGHER or LOWER.
  • Each correct prediction advances you one step up the Points Ladder: $1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 12, 17, 23, 30$ points.
  • Click BANK POINTS after any correct prediction to lock in that step's points for the round.
  • An incorrect prediction ends your round immediately with 0 points for that round.

🌐 Pass & Play vs. Online Multiplayer

  • Pass & Play: Players enter their names and take turns on one device, each receiving their own independent shuffled sequence of cards.
  • Online Multiplayer: All players receive the exact same sequence each round, and each next card is revealed simultaneously once everyone active has chosen (with a 10-second timer—players who take longer than 10 seconds automatically stay in the round rather than banking).

🧠 Expected Value (EV) Decision Rule

Suppose the current card is $x$, and $R$ unseen cards remain in the deck ($H$ cards greater than $x$, and $L = R - H$ cards less than $x$). The optimal prediction probability is:

$$p^* = \frac{\max(H, L)}{R}$$

At step $s$ with banked value $V_s$ and next step value $V_{s+1}$, continuing yields expected points $\mathbb{E}[\text{continue}] = p^* \cdot V_{s+1}$. Continuing has higher expected value than banking whenever:

$$p^* \cdot V_{s+1} > V_s \iff p^* > \frac{V_s}{V_{s+1}}$$
